# Publication visual sources

## Current explanatory flowcharts

- `pre-mechanism-flow.png`: adaptive phase frustration, collective-state feedback, numerical checks, and the scope of Ott-Antonsen reduction. Based on the PRE paper and author manuscript; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/8nxh-kpt8.
- `hybrid-forcing-mechanism-flow.png`: constant and state-dependent driving terms, the feedback mechanism, numerical and theoretical analysis, and entrainment-transition regimes. Based on the published article; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chaos.2026.118699.

These Chinese diagrams were created with the built-in imagegen tool and checked against the papers for formulas, feedback directions, and qualifications. They explain mechanisms and research methods; they are not original paper figures, simulated data, or quantitative results. The diagrams explicitly state this distinction. [Generation prompts](./DIAGRAM_PROMPTS.md) are retained for future revision.

## Original paper figures retained

The files below were used in an earlier profile layout and remain available. They were rendered from author-provided PDFs, with only surrounding page text and whitespace cropped away. Data, axes, legends, and panel layouts were preserved; these original figures were not regenerated from simulations or redrawn.

### PRE: adaptive phase frustration

- File: `pre-figure-1-author-manuscript.png`
- Source: author manuscript, page 2, Figure 1.
- Paper: Junyi Zheng, Changgui Gu, Jiangsheng Wang, and Yuxuan Song, *Hysteretic synchronization transitions induced by adaptive phase frustration*, Physical Review E 114, 054206 (2026).
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/8nxh-kpt8
- Figure: forward/backward scans and theoretical branches for the order parameter and effective phase frustration.
- The profile labels this as an author-manuscript figure. The image has not been compared pixel-for-pixel with the publisher's final layout.

### Hybrid forcing control

- File: `hybrid-forcing-figure-3.png`
- Source: published article PDF, page 5, Figure 3.
- Paper: Junyi Zheng, Changgui Gu, Jiangsheng Wang, and Guolin Wu, *Hybrid forcing control of entrainment transitions*, Chaos, Solitons & Fractals 210, 118699 (2026).
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chaos.2026.118699
- Figure: global order-parameter phase diagrams showing incoherent, entrained, and hysteretic regions.

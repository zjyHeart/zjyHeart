# Publication visual sources

## Current monochrome mechanism banners

- `pre-mechanism-mono-clear.png`: adaptive phase frustration and the reinforcing collective-state feedback under beta > 0 and 0 < alpha_0 < pi/2. Based on the PRE paper and author manuscript; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/8nxh-kpt8.
- `hybrid-forcing-mechanism-mono-clear.png`: same-phase constant and state-dependent driving terms, with coherence feedback routed only to the state-dependent term. Based on the published article; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chaos.2026.118699.

These Chinese diagrams were created with the built-in imagegen tool and checked against the papers for formulas, feedback directions, and qualifications. These minimal-text banners use a black/white/gray line-art style and an exact 3:1 aspect ratio and explain the core mechanisms; they are not original paper figures, simulated data, or quantitative results. This source note identifies this distinction; the images have no top-right explanatory label. [Generation prompts](./DIAGRAM_PROMPTS_CLEAR.md) are retained for future revision.

## Earlier explanatory design retained

The first monochrome banners remain as `pre-mechanism-mono-banner.png` and `hybrid-forcing-mechanism-mono-banner.png`, with prompts in [DIAGRAM_PROMPTS_MONO.md](./DIAGRAM_PROMPTS_MONO.md). The current revision removes the top-right label, uses distinct PRE phase-vector/phase-lag/phase-attraction graphics, and separates the hybrid driving path from the dashed state-feedback path. The hybrid oscillator symbol is a phasor ensemble, not a directed ring network.

The more detailed dark diagrams remain as `pre-mechanism-tech.png` and `hybrid-forcing-mechanism-tech.png`, with their prompts in [DIAGRAM_PROMPTS_TECH.md](./DIAGRAM_PROMPTS_TECH.md).

The earlier light-background diagrams remain as `pre-mechanism-flow.png` and `hybrid-forcing-mechanism-flow.png`, with their original prompts in [DIAGRAM_PROMPTS.md](./DIAGRAM_PROMPTS.md). Those earlier dark designs use conceptual oscillator-phase graphics. The current monochrome banners retain only the core equation and feedback mechanism; scientific qualifications are recorded above and in the image alt text.

## Original paper figures retained

The files below were used in an earlier profile layout and remain available. They were rendered from author-provided PDFs, with only surrounding page text and whitespace cropped away. Data, axes, legends, and panel layouts were preserved; these original figures were not regenerated from simulations or redrawn.

### PRE: adaptive phase frustration

- File: `pre-figure-1-author-manuscript.png`
- Source: author manuscript, page 2, Figure 1.
- Paper: Junyi Zheng, Changgui Gu, Jiangsheng Wang, and Yuxuan Song, *Hysteretic synchronization transitions induced by adaptive phase frustration*, Physical Review E 114, 054206 (2026).
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/8nxh-kpt8
- Figure: forward/backward scans and theoretical branches for the order parameter and effective phase frustration.
- The profile labels this as an author-manuscript figure. The image has not been compared pixel-for-pixel with the publisher's final layout.

### Hybrid forcing control

- File: `hybrid-forcing-figure-3.png`
- Source: published article PDF, page 5, Figure 3.
- Paper: Junyi Zheng, Changgui Gu, Jiangsheng Wang, and Guolin Wu, *Hybrid forcing control of entrainment transitions*, Chaos, Solitons & Fractals 210, 118699 (2026).
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chaos.2026.118699
- Figure: global order-parameter phase diagrams showing incoherent, entrained, and hysteretic regions.
